What it does

Everything a small line needs — nothing it doesn't

Built for factories that cannot afford a dedicated vision integrator, a GPU, or months of labelling.

◐

Normal-only training

PatchCore builds a memory bank of normal appearance from 20–30 good photos. No defect examples, no labels, a working model in seconds.

◎

Explainable verdicts

Every decision ships with a heatmap, 3×3 defect location, area, intensity and a plain-language explanation — never just a score.

⬡

Learned component check

A second signal catches missing coloured components — like a peeled label on a transparent bottle — even when the anomaly score alone would pass.

▤

Phone camera as a sensor

Pair an Android phone once and stream live frames into the Inspect tab or the multi-camera grid. Frames stay on the LAN.

⌗

Tunable, reviewable

The supervisor tunes the threshold with an impact preview; borderline units go to REVIEW and the operator's disposition is recorded next to the system verdict.

⛨

Teams & audit trail

Organizations, invitations and roles live in the admin console, isolated with row-level security. Every inspection is logged with its images and synced.

From photos to a live station

Five steps, about an afternoon

  1. 01

    Onboard

    Capture 20–30 good units, approve one product mask. A product profile is created on the factory machine.

  2. 02

    Train

    PatchCore builds a memory bank of normal appearance in seconds — the component check is calibrated from the same photos.

  3. 03

    Inspect

    Live frames from a webcam, an upload or a paired phone; every patch is compared against the memory bank.

  4. 04

    Decide

    Score + tunable threshold → PASS / REVIEW / FAIL with heatmap, location and explanation.

  5. 05

    Log & sync

    Inspections are stored locally with evidence, then synced idempotently to the cloud for the KPI dashboard.
